About Me

ML Engineer & Data Scientist

I view Data Science and Machine Learning not merely as writing code, but as a strategic tool to solve complex business problems and drive actionable decisions. While automation handles generic development, my focus lies in the high value architecture: defining clear problem statements, auditing data pipelines for leakage, and translating raw outputs into business clarity

My approach prioritizes model explainability and system transparency. Rather than building "black-box" models, I engineer solutions where stakeholders can clearly understand why a model makes a prediction whether that involves evaluating classification metrics, analyzing feature importance, or identifying high opportunity targets in large scale datasets. I thrive at the intersection of technical engineering and strategic communication. I don't just sit behind a desk writing scripts. I collaborate with teams to identify operational bottlenecks, design end-to-end analytical solutions, and deliver data-backed insights that drive real-world impact.
